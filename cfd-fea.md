# Computational Fluid Dynamics & FEA

This section highlights my computational research evaluating fluid behaviors, aerodynamics, and structural integrity using advanced numerical solvers.

## Computational Fluid Dynamics (ANSYS Fluent)

* **Axial Turbine Rotating Tower Analysis:** Conducted comprehensive CFD modeling of a rotating architectural tower utilizing an axial turbine concept. The simulation workflow involved configuring dynamic mesh behavior and 6-DOF rotational aerodynamics to capture fluid-structure wall interactions. I also evaluated atmospheric wind speed distributions at varying elevation heights to assess the tower's aerodynamic efficiency and power generation potential.
* **Microfluidic Droplet Simulation:** Modeled multiphase microfluidic droplet formation and transport behaviors to analyze precise fluid flow characteristics at the micro-scale. 

## Finite Element Analysis (ANSYS Mechanical)

* **Vibrating Screen Machine Analysis:** Executed deep mechanical evaluations of heavy vibrating screen equipment within the ANSYS Workbench environment. This included performing comprehensive modal, static structural, harmonic response, and transient structural modeling. I specifically formulated spring stiffness calculations and conducted dynamic load simulations to accurately capture the structural responses of the machinery under operational stress.
