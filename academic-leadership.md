# Academic Leadership

Beyond direct research and simulation work, I have extensive experience mentoring engineering students and guiding them through foundational and advanced mechanical engineering coursework.

## Teaching Assistantships

* **Advanced Robotics:** Directed project workflows and evaluated technical deliverables for advanced robotics coursework. Responsibilities included grading complex kinematics derivations, checking Denavit-Hartenberg and Craig convention frame assignments, and identifying inverse kinematics errors. I also guided student groups through state configurations and the rigorous validation of MuJoCo physics simulations.
* **Mechanical Vibrations:** Mentored students in the analysis of oscillatory systems, helping them bridge the gap between theoretical vibration models and practical engineering applications.
* **Ordinary Differential Equations:** Supported foundational mathematical instruction, assisting students in solving and applying differential equations to real-world physical systems and dynamic models.
